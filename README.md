Give Old Items a New Life

RE HOME is a sustainable refurbished-products platform based on the Buy → Repair → Refurbish → Sell business model. The platform focuses on giving used and unused products a second life by repairing and improving their condition and making them available to customers at affordable prices.

Our project covers a wide range of products such as electronics, furniture, household appliances, and other reusable items.

🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of RE HOME is to create a sustainable business model that benefits both customers and the environment.

The project aims to:

Reduce waste from usable products.
Extend the life cycle of old products.
Provide affordable alternatives to expensive new products.
Create opportunities for repair and refurbishment workers.
Build a trusted platform for buying refurbished products.
Promote reuse instead of unnecessary disposal.
🚨 Problem Statement

Many products that are still usable are discarded because of minor damages, outdated appearance, or lack of proper repair facilities.

At the same time, customers may want products at lower prices but cannot afford brand-new items.

RE HOME addresses both problems by collecting suitable used products, repairing them, checking their quality, and reselling them at reasonable prices.

💡 Proposed Solution

RE HOME follows a four-stage process:

1. BUY

We identify and purchase suitable used products from customers or other sources.

2. REPAIR

Products are inspected and necessary repairs are carried out by technicians.

3. REFURBISH

The products are cleaned, improved, tested, and prepared for resale.

4. SELL

The refurbished products are offered to customers at affordable prices.

Buy → Repair → Refurbish → Sell

⚖️ Break-Even Analysis

The project estimates break-even based on the fixed costs and profit contribution from each product.

Fixed Cost: ₹85,000
Profit per Product: ₹1,000

Break-Even = ₹85,000 ÷ ₹1,000 = 85 products/year

This is approximately 7–8 products per month.

The business aims to reach break-even within approximately 4–5 months under the projected sales assumptions.

⚙️ Operational Plan

The operational process begins with identifying suitable used products.

After purchasing, every product goes through inspection. Damaged products are sent to the repair team. Once repaired, products are cleaned, tested, and checked for quality.

After quality approval, the product is priced according to its condition, repair cost, and market demand.

Finally, the product is promoted through the website and social media platforms and delivered to customers after purchase.
